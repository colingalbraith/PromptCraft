Review as You Type

A small badge in the chat box scores your draft and points out its weakest spots, each with a one-click fix. It runs on your device; nothing is sent until you ask.

Suggest, Then Accept

The rewrite streams into a card beside the box, and your text changes only when you accept. Shorter, more detail, compare word by word, or undo.

Knows the Conversation

Builds on the chat you're in, and leaves a [blank] with a field to fill in wherever only you know the detail. No invented facts.

Any Model You Like

Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, any OpenAI-compatible API, a local model with Ollama, or the model built into your browser with no key at all.

Templates & Tones

Fourteen starting points for code, writing, research and planning, five tones, and your own presets. All in a side panel beside any page.

Private by Design

No servers, accounts or tracking. Keys stay on your device, and with Ollama or the built-in model your prompts never leave your computer.

Install in a minute.

PromptCraft isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it installs from GitHub. Chrome 116 or newer.

  1. Download

    Get the promptcraft-<version>.zip file from the latest release, then unzip it.

    Download

  2. Open your extensions

    Paste this into Chrome's address bar. Websites can't link to it, so it needs copying.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    It's the switch in the top right corner of that page.

  4. Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Then click the PromptCraft icon in the toolbar: a short setup asks which model to use.

Questions?

Is PromptCraft free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT License, with no account and no limits. The AI provider you pick may charge for its API: Gemini has a free tier, OpenAI and Claude are paid, and Ollama and the model built into your browser cost nothing.

Where do my prompts go?

Nowhere until you ask for a rewrite. Then your draft, and the recent conversation if you allow it, go straight from your browser to the provider you chose. PromptCraft has no servers. With Ollama or the built-in model, nothing leaves your computer. The privacy policy has the details.

Which model should I use?

If setup offers Built-in, start there: no key, no cost, and it runs on your computer. Otherwise Gemini has a free API key and gives good rewrites. Claude and OpenAI write the strongest prompts, and Ollama runs any model locally.

Does it work offline?

Yes, with Ollama or the built-in model once it has downloaded. The live review of your draft always runs on your device.

Which sites does it work on?

The badge appears in the prompt box on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Grok, HuggingFace and OpenRouter. On any other site, right-click a text field and choose Enhance with PromptCraft, or press Ctrl+Shift+E (⌘⇧E on a Mac).

Will it change my text without asking?

No. The rewrite appears in a card beside the box, and your text changes only when you press Accept. Undo puts your draft back.

Review as You Type

A small badge in the chat box scores your draft and points out its weakest spots, each with a one-click fix. It runs on your device; nothing is sent until you ask.

Suggest, Then Accept

The rewrite streams into a card beside the box, and your text changes only when you accept. Shorter, more detail, compare word by word, or undo.

Knows the Conversation

Builds on the chat you're in, and leaves a [blank] with a field to fill in wherever only you know the detail. No invented facts.

Any Model You Like

Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, any OpenAI-compatible API, a local model with Ollama, or the model built into your browser with no key at all.

Templates & Tones

Fourteen starting points for code, writing, research and planning, five tones, and your own presets. All in a side panel beside any page.

Private by Design

No servers, accounts or tracking. Keys stay on your device, and with Ollama or the built-in model your prompts never leave your computer.
